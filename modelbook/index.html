The closet she
opened for me.

The day before my flight back to college, my mom took me to a closet she had kept for decades. It was full of her clothes from her own college years, in the 2000s. She had great fashion sense. She told me to pick whatever I wanted.

I know I’ll always have her beside me, and I know how much she has sacrificed for me to be who I am today. That’s what drives me, on the runway and off it.

My motivation is my mom.

A runway concept for Fashion Etc.

Borrowed Wings.

Walk it · Direct it · Run the numbers

Every look starts in someone else’s closet. A mom’s, a sister’s, a roommate’s. My mom opened hers to me. This show opens everyone’s.

Each model walks in something borrowed from a person who shaped them, styled up to full runway glamour, with wings we make by hand. The lenders sit in the front row. In the finale, the wings come off.

Act IBlush

Soft. Satin, knits, pearls, small wings.

Blush wash · slow R&B

Act IICrimson

Power. 2000s tailoring from our parents’ closets, red lip.

Crimson side light · house, one V-pop remix

Act IIIMidnight

Night. Black lace, sequins, gold, the biggest wings.

One white follow spot · 2000s pop remix

FinaleOff duty

Everyone in their own white tee and jeans, wings in hand.

House lights up · one song

One straight white runway, audience on both sides, a photo pit at the end. A floor mirror marks the turn: each model stops, holds a flash beat, and turns. Cast for a range of bodies, genders and walks. About 20 minutes.
